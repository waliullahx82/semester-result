# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and classmates checking the SUST CSE 1-2 semester results from phones and laptops. The primary job is to find an individual registration quickly, understand the complete result, and compare only records with genuinely comparable course coverage.

## Product Purpose

CSE 1-2 Result Explorer turns the supplied course-wise PDFs and CSV result sheets into a searchable, auditable semester view with individual results, fair rankings, course analytics, and direct links back to every source. Success means a student can verify every displayed grade against its source and can clearly see when data is unofficial, incomplete, or conflicted.

## Brand Personality

Clear, confident, lively. The product should feel student-led and current while remaining calm enough for high-stakes academic information.

## Anti-references

- Dense legacy university portals with weak hierarchy and desktop-only tables.
- Game-like leaderboards, neon effects, glass panels, or celebratory visuals that trivialize results.
- Generic card grids, excessive decoration, and motion that delays access to information.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before decoration: every result retains visible source provenance.
- Comparable means fair: never rank incomplete and full-semester records together.
- Uncertainty stays visible: unofficial, missing, and conflicted data is never silently normalized.
- Search first: the shortest path from registration number to understandable result wins.
- Calm density: show rich academic data without turning the interface into a wall of tables.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA with complete keyboard access, visible focus, semantic tables and headings, 4.5:1 minimum text contrast, non-color status cues, readable chart alternatives, and reduced-motion support. The interface must work from 360px mobile widths through large desktops.
